How it works

Four moves, from an idea to a finished palette.

DescribeType any idea in the top bar (night skies, 1970s desert motel, premium cold brew) and press Return.
PickFour directions appear. Click the one that feels right; the others stay one click away.
RefineClick any swatch to edit it. Lock the colors you love, press Space to reshuffle the rest, ⌘Z to undo.
Take itSpec sheet for a PDF, .ase for Illustrator and Photoshop, CSS for the web. Save palette keeps it in your Library.
Keep it handyChrome: click Install app in the top bar. Safari: File › Add to Dock. iPhone: Share › Add to Home Screen.
